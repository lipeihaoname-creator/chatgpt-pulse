## 生成提示词

### 初始表情（用户参考图 4、6、7、9）

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent anime expression sprite atlas for the local ChatGPT Pulse desktop companion. Primary request: create one polished production-ready SQUARE transparent PNG, exactly 3 EQUAL COLUMNS by 3 EQUAL ROWS, nine matching head-and-shoulder portraits of Roxy Migurdia (ロキシー / 洛琪希) from Mushoku Tensei. Input images 1-4 are visual identity, expression, costume and anime style references, not edit targets; do not copy their scenes or watermarks. Subject: recognizable Roxy, soft blue-violet hair with her distinctive swept bangs, small ahoge, two braided lengths visible by the shoulders, blue-violet eyes, fully clothed tan travel-mage cloak with cream high collar and black trim. She is WITHOUT her hat in this atlas, matching the user's hatless references, so the expressive face fills the small circular avatar. Style: refined TV-anime cel shading, crisp yet delicate outlines, gentle warm skin, subtle eye highlights, normal anime head proportions (not chibi), consistent face and costume. Near frontal view with a tiny three-quarter turn, SAME portrait position, head scale, silhouette, lighting and shoulder framing in all nine equal square cells. Each stays completely inside its own equal cell with 8 percent transparent safety margin; no portrait crosses a cell. Exact expression order left to right, top to bottom: [0 calm reserved small smile; 1 attentive serious teacher focus; 2 thoughtful slightly raised gaze with pensive brows; 3 friendly explaining with small open mouth; 4 determined concentrating on a difficult spell, composed; 5 gently inviting a response, inquisitive brows; 6 warm satisfied proud teacher smile with relaxed open eyes; 7 mildly puzzled uncertain small frown, not panicked; 8 worried but composed, small concerned brows]. Vary eyebrows, eyes and mouth clearly, retain head and hair alignment. All nine have both eyes open for base frame. Nine distinct readable expressions at 88px display. Everything outside portraits must be truly transparent alpha, no painted checkerboard, no background circles. No text, captions, numbers, grid lines, staff, hands, logos or watermark. This is ONE precisely aligned 3x3 equal-cell sprite atlas, not a collage of different crops.

### 统一透明边距与边缘检查（输入初始表情）

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached transparent 3x3 Roxy Migurdia expression sprite atlas. Preserve all nine faces, expressions, blue-violet hair, braids, cream collar, tan cloak, exact colors and anime artwork. Make a production cleanup: remove ALL blue/red/yellow/white speckles, jagged colored halos, detached pixels and artifacts in transparent areas and along cutout outlines. Reframe each portrait uniformly slightly smaller so each fits fully into its equal square cell with a CLEAR TRANSPARENT 6 percent gap on top, left and right and a 3 percent gap at bottom. Exactly three equal columns and three equal rows on the same square canvas. In every cell the hair tuft and the shoulders must stay within the cell, never touch any other portrait. Nine portrait centers and sizes must be identical across cells. Retain the exact same facial expressions and eye states (all open). Crisp clean cel shaded edges, natural anti-aliasing, genuinely transparent alpha outside portraits, no black/white/checkerboard painted background, no extra decorative dots, no lines, text or watermark. Do not change the character or expressions; this is edge cleanup and equal cell margin correction only.

### 闭眼帧（输入清理后的 open.png）

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: this exact transparent 3x3 Roxy Migurdia character expression atlas. Create the CLOSED EYELID animation frame, not a new design. Preserve the exact square canvas dimensions, 3 equal columns and 3 equal rows; all nine head positions, scale, hair silhouette, braids, ahoge, costume, mouth expressions, eyebrows, lighting, colors and transparent margins unchanged. Change ONLY both eyes in each cell to naturally fully closed fine anime eyelid/lash lines. Do not lower eyebrows or rotate/move the heads. No new sleepy expression, blush or smile. Same mouth in every corresponding cell. The output will overlay the original at the same coordinates during a fast blink, so exact alignment of face/hair/shoulders is crucial. Everything outside existing portraits truly transparent alpha. No painted checkerboard, text, labels, decorations or watermark. Retain all 9 portraits at exactly the input coordinates; eyes closed is the only visual change.

### 半闭过渡帧（输入 open.png 和 blink.png）

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image 1 is the exact OPEN eye Roxy Migurdia 3x3 transparent sprite atlas; input image 2 is its CLOSED eyelid companion. Create the single HALF-CLOSED eyelid frame between them. Use the exact same square canvas with exactly 3 equal columns and 3 equal rows, all nine head/costume/hair silhouettes, position, size, mouths, eyebrows, shadows, colors and transparent margins exactly as input 1. Change ONLY upper eyelids in all nine portraits: lower them halfway toward the closed lash lines in input 2, with about half of each blue-violet iris still visible. This is a fraction-of-a-second blink frame, NOT a sleepy or bored redesign. No eyebrow, mouth, head angle or hair movement. Do not re-center, zoom, crop, relight or move shoulders. True transparent alpha outside portraits. No captions, grid lines, extra dots, symbols or watermark. One intermediate 3x3 frame atlas, aligned to input 1, not new portraits.
